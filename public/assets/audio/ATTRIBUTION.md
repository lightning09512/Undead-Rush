# Audio asset credits

## Zombies

`zombie-1.wav` through `zombie-24.wav` come from **Zombies Sound Pack** by artisticdude, released as CC0. The pack contains 24 zombie sounds created for Summoning Wars.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack

## Firearms

The source recordings for `weapons/cz.wav`, `weapons/sks.wav`, and `weapons/shotty.wav` come from **Gunshot Sounds**, submitted by Tabasco. The OpenGameArt page marks the pack CC0. The included archive also contains a notice naming Vincent Sevedge and CC BY 3.0; that notice is retained here for attribution: “Copyright (c) 2009 Vincent Sevedge”.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
License page: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

## Reloads

`reload/rifle-reload.wav` is from **Gun reload sounds** by SpringySpringo, CC0. The original recordings use airsoft guns.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds

`reload/shotgun-first-shell.mp3`, `reload/shotgun-shells.mp3`, and `reload/shotgun-rack.mp3` are from **Shotgun Reload Sound effects** by zer0_sol, CC0.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects
